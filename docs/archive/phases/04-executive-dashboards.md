# Phase 4: Executive Dashboards

## Goal

Create decision-ready dashboards that show how operational data becomes management insight.

## Dashboard 1: President Overview

Widgets:

- Monthly revenue.
- Gross margin.
- Fleet utilization.
- Vessel availability.
- Jobs completed and delayed.
- Open receivables.
- Maintenance downtime.
- HSE open actions.
- Crew document risks.
- Document expiries.

Decision questions:

- Are we profitable this month?
- Which vessels need attention?
- Which customers or jobs are hurting cash flow?
- Are there safety or compliance risks that can stop operations?

## Dashboard 2: Operations

Widgets:

- Today's dispatch board.
- Job status counts.
- Delayed jobs by reason.
- Vessel assignment status.
- Fuel consumption by job and vessel.
- Standby and waiting time.

Decision questions:

- Can we accept another job today?
- Which jobs are at risk?
- Which vessel should be dispatched next?
- Are we capturing billable waiting time?

## Dashboard 3: Fleet And Maintenance

Widgets:

- Vessel availability.
- Work orders by severity.
- Preventive maintenance due soon.
- Downtime by vessel.
- Maintenance cost by vessel.
- Critical spare shortages.

Decision questions:

- Which vessel is most likely to fail operations?
- Which repairs should be prioritized?
- What parts need urgent purchasing?

## Dashboard 4: Finance

Widgets:

- Revenue by customer.
- Gross margin by vessel.
- AR aging.
- Unbilled completed jobs.
- Fuel cost variance.
- Purchases pending approval.

Decision questions:

- Where is cash stuck?
- Which customers should be followed up?
- Which jobs are complete but not billed?
- Are costs eroding margins?

## Dashboard 5: Compliance And HSE

Widgets:

- Incidents and near misses.
- Open corrective actions.
- Expiring vessel certificates.
- Expiring crew certificates and medicals.
- Audit findings by status.

Decision questions:

- What can stop us from operating legally or safely?
- Are corrective actions being closed on time?
- Which vessel or department has the most risk?

## Deliverables

- Dashboard wireframes or working dashboard pages.
- KPI definitions.
- Sample drill-down paths.
- Demo-ready charts and tables.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every KPI has a defined calculation.
- Every major KPI can drill down into sample records.
- The president overview can tell the business story in one screen.
- Dashboards expose both performance and risk.
