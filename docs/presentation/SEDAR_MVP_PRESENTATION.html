The opportunity
01

From separate records to one operating picture

When information is separated

  • Teams maintain their own files and versions
  • The same details are encoded more than once
  • Approvals and follow-ups are hard to trace
  • Management learns about risk too late
  • Profit requires manual consolidation

When the business is connected

  • Enter once and share with the right teams
  • See owners, status, reasons, and history
  • Surface exceptions before deadlines
  • Move completed work into billing faster
  • See margin by job, vessel, and customer
The goal is not simply to digitize forms. It is to give SEDAR a clearer, safer, and more accountable way to run the business.

Solution fit
Tools working together

One core, with the right specialist connections

◎

Odoo core

Shared operational backbone: finance, purchasing, inventory, people, maintenance, documents, customer activity, and SEDAR tug workflows.

▥

Microsoft layer

Power BI for executive reporting; Microsoft 365 and Teams for collaboration; SharePoint for governed libraries.

↗

Specialist connections

DocuSign, AIS/GPS, barcode devices, bank/payroll services; ShipNet or ABS NS5 if future fleet complexity requires it.

The recommended approach balances capability, scalability, and cost: use a practical shared core, then connect specialized services where they add real value.
