# Phase 5: Demo Hardening And Handoff

## Goal

Prepare the MVP for presentation, feedback, and transition into post-deal discovery.

## Demo Hardening

Tasks:

- Polish navigation and labels.
- Validate the end-to-end demo story.
- Check sample data consistency.
- Remove dead-end screens.
- Add empty states where records are filtered out.
- Confirm that dashboards match source records.
- Make placeholder assumptions visible.

## Demo Script

Recommended flow:

1. Start with the president overview.
2. Open a delayed job from the operations dashboard.
3. Show the assigned tug, crew, fuel, and voyage log.
4. Complete or review the job and show billing impact.
5. Open the vessel profile and show maintenance, certificates, and profitability.
6. Show inventory or procurement risk tied to a maintenance work order.
7. Close with finance, HSE, and document expiry alerts.

## Handoff Materials

Create:

- MVP feature list.
- Known assumptions list.
- Sample data dictionary.
- Discovery questions for SEDAR.
- Post-deal implementation roadmap.
- Gap list between MVP and production ERP.

## Post-Deal Discovery Questions

Ask SEDAR for:

- Vessel master list and technical specifications.
- Customer list and contract/rate structures.
- Current job request, dispatch, and voyage log forms.
- Billing rules and invoice templates.
- Chart of accounts and reporting requirements.
- Crew roles, certifications, rotations, and payroll rules.
- Maintenance checklists, dry dock plans, and equipment hierarchy.
- Inventory item master, warehouses, and reorder policies.
- Procurement approval matrix.
- HSE forms, audit requirements, and regulatory reports.
- Document register and renewal owners.
- Existing Excel files and reports by department.

## Production Readiness Later

After deal closing, the project should move through:

- Process discovery.
- Data migration planning.
- Configuration and customization.
- Integrations.
- Security and roles.
- User acceptance testing.
- Training.
- Pilot rollout.
- Full rollout.
- Support and continuous improvement.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The MVP can be demoed without live company data.
- The team can explain what is real, what is sample, and what needs SEDAR confirmation.
- The demo proves cross-department integration.
- The handoff package creates a clear next step after the deal is closed.
