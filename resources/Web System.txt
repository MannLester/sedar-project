As President of SEDAR Tug Services, Inc., I firmly believe that the continued growth and competitiveness of our company require us to embrace digital transformation. The complexity of managing tug operations can no longer rely on disconnected systems, manual processes, or separate Excel files maintained by individual departments.
 
Our vision is to implement a fully integrated Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Marine Fleet Management System that will unify all core business functions into a single platform. This system will seamlessly connect Finance, Tug Operations, Technical Maintenance, Crewing, Health, Safety, Security and Environment (HSSE), Procurement, Inventory Management, Human Resources, Marketing, and Executive Management.
 
With a centralized system, information will be entered once and shared across the organization in real time, providing a single source of truth for decision-making. This will eliminate duplicate data entry, improve operational efficiency, strengthen internal controls, enhance regulatory compliance, and provide management with accurate, timely, and reliable information.
 
Our objective is not simply to automate existing processes but to build a modern, data-driven organization capable of supporting sustainable growth, delivering world-class marine services, and meeting the evolving needs of our clients and stakeholders. Through this transformation, SEDAR Tug Services, Inc. will establish itself as one of the most professionally managed and technologically advanced tug service providers in the Philippines.
 
This initiative represents a strategic investment in the future of our company—one that will strengthen operational excellence, improve financial transparency, optimize asset utilization, enhance customer satisfaction, and reinforce our commitment to safety, reliability, and corporate governance.

My recommendation for SEDAR
 
Given SEDAR's size and our plans to strengthen corporate governance and internal controls, I would recommend:
 
Odoo as the core ERP because it provides accounting, procurement, inventory, HR, maintenance, and document management at a reasonable cost and can be customized for tug operations.
Add a dedicated maritime maintenance and fleet module if your fleet grows or maintenance needs become more complex.
Use Power BI for executive dashboards so the Board and CEO can monitor financial performance, fleet utilization, maintenance status, HSE metrics, and profitability in real time.
This combination offers a practical balance between capability, scalability, and cost for a tugboat operator like SEDAR.
